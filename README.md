# Desktop Kitty

 A little kitty I made to run around your screen and chase a ball!

It will run around and chase your mouse from the bottom of the screen. You can pick it up and drag it around. Hover over the kitty to make it sleep. If you double click on the kitty, you'll spawn a little ball for it to chase that you can also drag around. Double click again to make it disappear.

I know there is a little bit of a bug where the cat will glitch between sleeping and walking after you double click a second time to make the ball disappear, and I plan on fixing it shortly.
